Facts from the L1 spec draft (docs/processing/spec_detection_image_v1.md, scripts analysis/processing/detection_image_{scale_survey,period_check}.py):

- Carrier period must be measured on the background-removed (step 4) section with the AIHub estimator (trace-mean |rfft| peak). Raw DC-removed data is biased long: 4TU 3.1 vs 2.51 ns (+24 %), Guangzhou 400 MHz up to 2.5x; the Guangzhou header `spectrum_peak_MHz` is unusable (p10-p90 2.9-14 ns). 4TU P = 2.51 ns both whole-line and at true apexes (matches +-lobe spacing 1.2-1.4 ns = half period).
- AIHub tpipe apex crops P_px p50 4.06 (band crops 4.20). tpipe width = 11.4 P; 4TU/Guangzhou hyperbolas are 8-15 P wide, so matching P puts them at AIHub size. AIHub box shape is only consistent with near-isotropic depth units (horizontal compression <= 1.6x) [estimate].
- Native 1 trace = 1 sample images stretch hyperbolas vertically 2-8x vs depth-isotropic -> w/h 0.15-0.69, i.e. AIHub cavity (0.74) / manhole (0.54) shapes. Scale rule: s_y = P_line/P*, s_x = s_y dt v/(2 dx) (45 deg asymptote).
- GPR-SD dominant period ~51 samples (whole line and pipe-mark windows) = 62/73 MHz under dt hyp A/B, contradicting the documented 200-400 MHz; with v dt/2 = 0.012 m it implies ~80 MHz. Hyperbolas only 1-2.6 P wide -> no scale matches AIHub. Exploratory, not a verdict.
  - (2026-11-26 M3-6 plan pilot, analysis/processing/m36_gprsd_pilot*.py) The 51-sample peak is a low-frequency component, not the carrier: every road also has a second spectral peak at 14.4-14.8 samples (rel. amp p50 0.6, 29 % of power at periods < 20) = 227/268 MHz under dt A/B, inside the documented 200-400 MHz. Visual bands/apex ringing match ~14.6. r0 `measure_period_samples` searches up to n_s/4 = 80 samples, so it picks 51. L1 scale is dt-free (s_y = P/P*, s_x = s_y dz/dx): dt A/B only changes eps_r, P and dz (curvature, ∝ dx) decide the image. dz 0.012 in the GPR-SD json is hard-coded in the provider script (no fit provenance). t0 open: sample 0 has full RMS (no quiet pre-trigger), CementRoad2 keeps a ~17-sample-period mean-trace wavelet peaking at sample 16-18 (direct-wave remnant or slab bottom), front-crop B could put t0 at negative samples; t0 and dz are coupled in the hyperbola fit (fit tau_z jointly on small point diffractors, R confound -R/dz samples).
  - (2026-11-26 M3-6 main diagnostics, analysis/processing/m36_gprsd_*.py) Outside label windows the 14.6 peak is a horizontally coherent component: after moving-median (101 tr) removal the spectrum is a 14-30 plateau; apex A-scans give P ~19-22 samples (zero-crossing 19.3, gated spectrum 20.5-22.3, quantised by nfft 512 at ~4 %/bin). The 51 component is ~90 % horizontally coherent (processing/ringing). Automatic hyperbola picks on this processed data are contaminated by quasi-flat undulating events: dz from curvature depends on QC (plan QC 0.020 truncated by a phys band, strict QC 0.030) and on tau_z; tracker is unbiased on synthetics (<1.5 %), so use migration-focus scans (Stolt) or manual picks, not median of auto picks. tau_z profile with free per-diffractor dz is flat until the shallowest-apex cap -> not identifiable. Only the ratio dx/(v dt) is measured; L1 needs only g = dx/dz (dx- and dt-free).
  - (2026-11-26 original .mat check, analysis/processing/m36_gprsd_mat_inspect.json) The public originals (ubriustc/GPR_data split zip, stored entries, MAT v5 compressed) hold ONLY variable `P` (321 x N double) - no time axis/dt/zero/header fields; package npy == float32(P) bit-exact. So 321 is the provider's published count (380/97 ns README also misdescribes "3 images per mat"); the .mat route to t0/dt A-B is exhausted - only Zhou 2018 text or provider contact remain. Split zip = plain byte split: concatenate parts, zipfile works (no 7-Zip on this PC).
- `detect/base.py letterbox` upscales small inputs (ratio = min(256/H, 256/W)); keep scale by padding inside L1 (right/bottom, maps outside the grid -> clipped).
- Overlap lower bound must be capped in periods, not only metres: in depth-isotropic L1, lambda/2 = P* px, and the widest AIHub tpipe box is ~20 P (80.7 px). A 2 a_max = 2 m bound blows up for high-frequency lines (Guangzhou 2000 MHz 325 px). Per-line eps also matters (4TU O_phys 64-96 px uncapped).
- Content height > 256 px does happen at default P*: Guangzhou MALA (dt 0.391 ns, 190-200 ns window = 40-80 periods). MALA measured period scatters 4.45-36.6 samples between folders (processed data), so counts depend on the period assumption (8 vs 75 lines).
- 4TU GT neighbour utility spacing p50 0.20 m (63 % <= 0.30 m) vs Fresnel radius 0.19-0.43 m: most neighbours are one GPR event anyway.

- (2026-11-14 M3-2 plan pilot, analysis/processing/m32_{baseline_texture,horizontal_coherence}.py) AIHub crops keep horizontally coherent energy (row-mean share R_h p50 0.23-0.40 by class, top 15 % rows 0.34-0.65) -> AIHub looks not (or weakly) background-removed; 4TU L1 after median bg R_h 0.017, and a longer window does not help (0.019) because most 4TU lines (<=301 traces) already use the whole-line median. AIHub decays with depth (bottom/top RMS 0.30-0.41) while AGC 8/f_c makes 4TU flat (0.91). 20/26 4TU S lines have H_c < 128 -> bottom pad taller than the trained pad band max 128 px; AIHub flat rows p50 1-2 %. AIHub whole-crop period (no band-pass) is 8-14 px (horizontal structure), so compare R2 periods only with identical definitions. 4TU dt 0.195: P_line min 12.15 samples vs compression threshold 11.98 (n_s P*/256.5), scale_out_of_range needs < 8.89 -> no issue. Keep period measurement on the fitting-branch (median 301) section even if the detection bg recipe changes. Full survey (m32_content_height.py, P* 6): 50 % of 4TU+IDS tiles have bottom pad > 128 px; Guangzhou IDS with MEASURED periods has 20 compressed lines but only 1 with c > 1.35 (spec's "2000 MHz 2 + 600 MHz 1" came from 1/f assumed periods). AIHub gain value = inverse contrast factor (gain 1.0 linear); no-bg/no-AGC evidence only for RA/NM equipment (pipes, manhole), MA (cavity) undecidable (curator m32_aihub_processing_facts.md). MALA 8x period spread is processing residue/harmonics, ~250 MHz antenna (EM m32_em_review.md).
- (2026-11-14 stage A, m32_texture_recipes.py) NM reference rho_bt is 0.21 (not the all-class 0.41). No bg removal overshoots R_h (4TU 0.50, IDS 0.94 vs AIHub NM 0.27: ground-coupled direct wave dominates); partial alpha 0.5-0.8 lands near for 4TU only. r0 distance is dominated by rho_bt (AGC). Capped AGC (beta 0.5) fixes rho_bt without touching R_h. Shortlist r0/r5/r6 depends on the domain-averaged D (pooled D would give r6/r7/r2) - fix such aggregation details in a committed script before running. tpow needs n ~1.8 (record time) to reach rho_bt 0.21. MALA at the real default: 45 compressed / 28 c>1.35 (spec's "75 all out of range" was wrong); 07011 folder has no 213 MHz peak (band max at 249 = edge) -> different antenna, not harmonic.

- (2026-11-15 stage C pre-check fail, m32_stageC_r0_signal_diag.py) Strong injected hyperbolas (18 dB) under r0: line p99 clip NOT pulled (ratio <= 1.07), median bg keeps ~99 % of apex; AGC 8/f_c saturates: wings lifted to apex level and in-window background suppressed -> wide strong A0 hyperbolas missed (EM: bg attenuation 0.68 -> 0.4). - (2026-11-15 M3-3 X6, m33_x6_period_snr.py) Morocco eval images have a coarse carrier: same whole-image estimator as P_A gives utilities P_in ~25.6 px (x6.3 P_A after letterbox 224->256), intact ~13.8, all 233 outside P_A x [0.55,1.5]; spectral power 20-36 px, nothing near 4-8 px. 4TU K apex SNR proxy (A = (1)-(4) apex env, sigma = MAD 1-3 m lateral) quartiles 11.8 / 18.5 / 20.9 dB -> most K apexes sit in the r0 AGC-saturation regime. AIHUB_WIDTH/HEIGHT_PERIODS come from m32_s1_recheck.py aihub_box_p (451 labels, rounded p5/p95).
- (2026-11-15 M3-3 C0a review, Y1 record-end cut) Re-measuring the period on a truncated gprMax record is unreliable: cut at apex + 0.5 P gives P_cut/P_full up to 3.8 (M04, M15 3.4; 9/20 > 1.4) or f_c fallback (10/20); 1 P: 10/20 off by > 10 %; 2-3 P within ~±11 %. Zero sample stays identical. When cutting a record to emulate record-end pipes, hold P from the full record; otherwise the image is rescaled and the cut in P_ell units becomes 0.13-0.5 instead of 0.5.

**Why:** these decide the L1 scale/tiling rule and are easy to get wrong by measuring on raw data or trusting headers.
**How to apply:** when verifying `DetectionImage` (M3-2) or judging cross-domain detection, re-measure P per line on bg-removed data; never treat L1 curvature as velocity. Related: [[band-depth-pitfalls]], [[fourtu-zero-band]].
